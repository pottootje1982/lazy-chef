# Suspicious product mappings — review list

Generated by `scripts/audit-mappings.ts` (read-only) over **1100 mappings**, then
hand-curated. Re-run with:

```
~/.nvm/versions/node/v22.17.1/bin/node --experimental-strip-types scripts/audit-mappings.ts
```

Two raw signals were computed: **high order count** (`defaultOrderCount ≥ 4`) and
**no word overlap** between the search term and the product name. The no-overlap
signal is noisy — many are correct Dutch substitutions (carrot→winterpeen, peas→
doperwten, green beans→haricots verts, onion→gele uien, white wine→a specific
wine). Those are filtered out below; what remains is grouped by severity.

---
